# RGX | Sonic The Hedgehog Level-Up! ## ![](https://github.com/RGXMods/SimpleQuestPlates/blob/main/media/kiwi.gif?raw=true) RGX Mods - RealmGX Community Project *** ## Overview **RGX | Sonic The Hedgehog Level-Up!** plays a Sonic-inspired level-complete chime whenever your character levels up in World of Warcraft. ![RealmGX Kiwi](https://github.com/RGXMods/SimpleQuestPlates/blob/main/media/kiwi.gif?raw=true) **The Kiwi Says:** "Gotta go fast! Bwwiiiee." *** ## Deprecation Notice This standalone addon is preserved for existing users. Its sound has also been merged into [BLU | Better Level Up!](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/betterlevelup) and [BLU Classic | Better Level Up!](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/blu-classic). *** ## Features Automatic playback - The custom sound plays when you gain a level. Default ding muting - WoW's default level-up sound is muted on login. Lightweight - Single-sound addon with no embedded libraries. Multi-Version - Includes support files for Retail, Wrath, Burning Crusade, and Classic Era. *** ## Command Reference No slash commands are required. Enable the addon and it works automatically. *** ## Installation 1. **Download** RGX | SHHLU from your preferred platform: * [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/sonicthehedgehoglevelup) * [Wago.io](https://addons.wago.io/addons/sonicthehedgehoglevelup) * [GitHub](https://github.com/RGXMods/SonicTheHedgehogLevelUp) 2. **Extract** the addon folder to your WoW `Interface/AddOns` directory. 3. **Restart** WoW and enable the addon. ![RealmGX Kiwi](https://github.com/RGXMods/SimpleQuestPlates/blob/main/media/kiwi.gif?raw=true) **The Kiwi Says:** "Installation complete. Bwwiiiee." *** ## 🛠️ Troubleshooting - If WoW marks STHLU as missing a dependency, install or enable `RGX-Framework`. - If no custom sound plays, run `/sthlu test`, then `/sthlu enable` and select a variant again. - If the default sound also plays, verify that STHLU and RGX-Framework both loaded without Lua errors. - If WoW cannot find the addon, verify the exact `Interface/AddOns/SonicTheHedgehogLevelUp` folder name. Because the standalone project is retired, migrate to BLU or BLU Classic when you prefer the consolidated sound addon. *** ## Thank you for choosing RGX Mods! **Made with care by the RealmGX Community** **Lead Developer:** [DonnieDice](https://github.com/donniedice) [Join our Discord Community](https://discord.gg/N7kdKAHVVF) | [Star us on GitHub](https://github.com/RGXMods/SonicTheHedgehogLevelUp) | [Support Development](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/donniedice) **💨 RGX Mods - Powered by RealmGX Community**
